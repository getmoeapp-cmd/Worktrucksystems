SMS Terms & Conditions

Effective August 17, 2026

These terms govern the WorkTruck Systems SMS program operated by WorkTruck Systems, Queens, NY.

Program description

When you opt in, we send text messages related to your inquiry or account: demo confirmations, appointment reminders, follow-ups, and customer support replies.

Opting in

You opt in by submitting a form on worktrucksystems.com that includes your phone number, or by providing your number to us during a sales conversation and agreeing to receive texts.

Message frequency & cost

Message frequency varies based on your activity. Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile carrier plan.

Opting out & help

Reply STOP to any message to cancel. After you send STOP, we will confirm your opt-out and send no further messages. Reply HELP for help, or contact support@worktrucksystems.com.

Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

Our privacy policy describes how we handle your information. Your mobile number and opt-in consent are never sold or shared with third parties for marketing.
