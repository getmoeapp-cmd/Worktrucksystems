Questions

The stuff people call to ask.

Do I need a permit?

Not if it goes in your driveway or on private property. On the street, NYC requires a DOT permit. Tell us when you book and we'll walk you through it — it's usually a day or two.

What can't go in the dumpster?

No tires, paint, batteries, refrigerators/AC units, propane tanks, asbestos, or hazardous liquids. Concrete, brick and dirt are fine but heavy — call us so we size it right.

How long can I keep it?

7 days are included. Need more? $15/day, just text us. No need to re-book.

What if I go over the weight limit?

Every load gets weighed at the transfer station. Over the included tonnage is $85/ton. You'll get a text with the ticket before we charge anything.

How fast can you deliver?

Next day almost always. Same day if you call before noon and we have a can free — check the live availability when you reserve.

Do I have to be there for delivery?

No. Tell us where you want it and we'll drop it. Send a photo of the spot if it's tight.
