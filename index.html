Frequently asked questions

The stuff you'd actually ask

When will I start seeing results?

Honest answer: it depends on how many calls and leads you already get. The system catches what's already coming at you — if the phone never rings, fix your advertising first and we'll make sure nothing leaks. If you think you can close your eyes and pay $297 a month to solve everything, we're probably not the right fit.

What we will promise: do you believe answering every missed call wins jobs? That replying to leads in seconds beats replying in hours? That confirmations cut no-shows, and asking every customer for a review builds more stars than asking when you remember? If yes — that's exactly what the system does, every day, without you touching it.

Why is it so cheap?

Because we'd rather keep you for years than squeeze you for months. Priced fair, month-to-month, no contract — if the system earns its keep, you stay. That's the whole business model.

Is the website really free?

Yes. It's included in the flat rate and typically live within about 72 hours — your name, services, pricing, and service area, with the quote form and phone number wired straight into the system.

Do I need to be good with computers?

If you can send a text, you can run this. Leads text you, the system texts them, and your job board updates itself. There's nothing to install and no dashboard homework.

Will it really text people back in the middle of the night?

The system replies the moment a call is missed — day or night, straight from the activity log. And if you'd rather your number keep quiet hours, that's a one-toggle change.

What happens if I cancel?

You cancel, we're bummed, nobody pays a fee. You lose access to the systems and the included website at the end of your month — that's it. No cancellation maze, no hostage negotiations.
